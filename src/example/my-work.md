## 1. The business

**What we do:** Larkfield Systems makes order management, route planning, and cold-chain compliance software for regional food distributors.

**Who we serve:** Independent and regional food distributors in Canada and the northern United States, running 40 to 400 trucks. The buyer is almost always a VP of Operations. The person who has to live with us is a dispatcher.

**How we make money:** Annual subscription priced per depot, plus a monthly fee per vehicle. Implementation is billed once at go-live, which is why late go-lives push revenue out of the quarter.

**Current scale:** $17.4M ARR. 118 people. 63 customers, 2,900 vehicles under management.

**Current strategic priority (this quarter):** Get net revenue retention above 105% before the Series B data room opens on March 20. Everything else this quarter is downstream of that number.

## 7. Decision tradeoffs

When the AI Chief of Staff has to choose between competing priorities in a brief, use these defaults:

1. **Revenue-protecting work** beats **new opportunity work**, unless the new opportunity has a deadline.
2. **External commitments** beat **internal optimization**, unless the internal item is on a deadline.
3. **Decisions only the leader can make** beat **work the leader could delegate**, every time.
4. **Strategic priority items** (section 2) beat **drift items** (section 5), every time.

Override these only when `tomorrow.md` says otherwise.
